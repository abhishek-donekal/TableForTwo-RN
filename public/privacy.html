Privacy Policy

Table for 2 · Last updated: August 13, 2026

Table for 2 ("the app") is a curated date-planning experience. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the app.

Information we collect

The current version of the app does not require an account and does not transmit personal information to our servers. Profile details, preferences, and date intents you enter are stored locally on your device.

Location

The app does not access your device's location. Safety features that reference location sharing operate on information you enter manually.

Payments

The iOS app does not process payments and contains no in-app purchases.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

Data retention & deletion

Data entered in the app remains on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Any live-location records used by safety features on our servers are automatically purged within 24 hours of a date window closing.

Children

Table for 2 is for users 18 and older.
